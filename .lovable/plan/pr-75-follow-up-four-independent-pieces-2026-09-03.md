## 3. Focus handling across all three reveals

`milestone-reveal.tsx`, `bought-pull-reveal.tsx` and `finish-celebration.tsx` each claim `aria-modal="true"` but only mark SiteNav inert, so Tab still reaches the page, and closing drops focus on `document.body`.

One shared hook (`src/hooks/use-modal-surface.ts`) used by all three, so two reveals that appear in the same flow cannot diverge. It:

- records `document.activeElement` on open and restores focus to it on close/unmount (guarding against an element that has since left the DOM),
- focuses the surface on mount,
- keeps Tab inside the surface with a wrap-around keydown trap rather than inerting the page root — the reveals render inside the same route subtree they would have to inert, and the app already has a working precedent for scoped inert on the nav only. The comment states that choice and why.

Returns a ref plus the props the surface spreads, so `finish-celebration`'s `motion.button` and the other two `div`s all work. No `role="button"` is added anywhere (`[role="button"][aria-pressed]` is the e2e card-on-stand selector), and the `bought-pull-done`, `milestone-done`, `milestone-reveal` and `bought-pull-reveal` testids stay exactly as they are.

Tests: extend `bought-pull-reveal.test.tsx` and `milestone-reveal.test.tsx` (which already assert `aria-modal`, `tabIndex` and focus), add focus-restore coverage to all three, and a Tab-containment case.

Growing it would cover the chip it annotates, so the separate trigger is deleted and the reactor names fold into the chip itself: the chip keeps its existing tap-to-react behaviour, gains an `aria-label`/`title` naming who reacted, and a long-press (pointer-hold, ~450ms, cancelled on move/leave) opens the same popover content anchored on the chip.

# Verification: trading

How to run this file: trading is the only part of the app where two collections
change at once, so **most of the interesting items need a second person on a
second handset** — two real member sessions, not two tabs, because a second tab
shares storage and would not exercise either the per-device dot or the
"first tap wins" race. Set up as follows. Bring the app up (`bun run dev` on a
phone on the same network, or the deployed app, which is the better surface for
anything realtime). Have a combine active with a roster and card art, so there
are spares to stake at all — outside an active combine both pickers are empty
and half this file is unrunnable. On **phone A**, claim a player with a paper
code and tear at least three packs, so you hold spares of several roster cards
and at least two secrets. On **phone B**, claim a _different_ player and do the
same. Keep a third browser with site data cleared for the two guest items
(`TRD-01`, `TRD-02`), and a commissioner session for the ten rows that need one —
`TRD-19`, `TRD-20`, `TRD-24`, `TRD-25`, `TRD-34`, `TRD-68`, `TRD-82`, `TRD-84`,
`TRD-85` and `TRD-87` — several of which need a card created, renamed or removed
before they can be run at all. Clear the Trade dot between items by clearing site
data only where an item says to — it also spends a fresh daily secret. Device
values
are as defined in [the protocol](README.md#devices-and-conditions); `second
person` in this file always means phone B driven by somebody else at the same
time, and rows carrying it cannot be run alone.

Not checkable by hand:

- That arriving costs six parallel requests. The document says the count was read
  from the hooks rather than measured, and several are usually cache hits from
  the vault ([Open questions](../trading/the-trading-post.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- Whether a real visitor reads the transient "Claim your player" panel or only
  the redirect. It is a race between an account lookup and a navigation, so the
  answer differs by connection rather than being a fixed behaviour; `TRD-02`
  records an observation, not a verdict.
- That no screen other than this one and the vault's pill links to the Trading
  Post. An absence across the whole source, not something a tester can see
  ([Open questions](../trading/the-trading-post.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That every write on this screen runs with full database privileges and bypasses
  row-level security. A property of the server, invisible from the phone
  ([Interactions with other systems](../trading/the-trading-post.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).

Not checkable by hand:

- Whether the counterparty's spares refresh quickly enough in practice to stop
  somebody staging a card that has just moved. The document says the thirty-second
  window was read from the cache settings and not measured at a party
  ([Open questions](../trading/making-an-offer.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That no third reason for a card being untradeable exists. The blocked row is
  built from two explicit cases rather than from the inverse of the rules, so its
  completeness is a property of the source, not of the screen
  ([Open questions](../trading/making-an-offer.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That the offer and its items are written as one thing or not at all. The
  document's own claim is that there is no state in which somebody has an offer
  with an empty table; a tester cannot provoke the half-written state to prove it
  ([While it runs](../trading/making-an-offer.md#while-it-runs)).
- That the nudge to the other side is capped at two seconds and its failure is
  swallowed. Observable only as an absence — the offer standing anyway — which is
  indistinguishable from the nudge having worked
  ([It settles](../trading/making-an-offer.md#it-settles)).

Not checkable by hand:

- That the summary is redacted once, at settle time, rather than being filtered on
  the way to a reader. The security property is in the ordering of two writes; what
  a tester sees is identical either way
  ([Interactions with other systems](../trading/the-trade-feed.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).
- That the stored summary contains no finish and no identifier of a copy. The
  document says this is asserted against real Postgres and has not been re-checked
  against the deployed database — a database read, not a screen
  ([Open questions](../trading/the-trade-feed.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That the load-bearing comment in the source points at a migration filename that
  does not exist. A source defect with no user-visible symptom
  ([Open questions](../trading/the-trade-feed.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- Whether a phone whose live channel dropped picks the feed up on the next window
  focus specifically, as opposed to on any of the other refetches the screen
  performs. The two are indistinguishable from the outside
  ([Open questions](../trading/the-trade-feed.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That no other screen in the app renders the completed-trades table. An absence
  across the source ([Open questions](../trading/the-trade-feed.md#open-questions-and-verification)).

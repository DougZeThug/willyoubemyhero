# Verification: dust

How to run this file: dust is member business and half of it is a market, so
**most of the interesting items need a second person on a second handset** — two
real member sessions, not two tabs, because the "exactly one of two simultaneous
buys succeeds" items and the seller-side poke items are the whole point of the
feature and a tab cannot stand in for a phone. Set up as follows. Bring the app
up (`bun run dev` on a phone on the same network, or the deployed app, which is
the better surface for anything realtime) with a combine active, a roster and
card art, so there are roster copies to mill at all. Have a **commissioner**
session in reach throughout: the dust switch is on the admin console and roughly
a quarter of this file turns on flipping it, so run the switch items in a block
rather than toggling between every row. On **phone A**, claim a player, tear
enough packs to hold at least three copies of one roster card (ideally one of
them gold or platinum) and at least two secrets from different days; then get a
balance onto it by milling one spare, so the pill has a number in it. On **phone
B**, claim a *different* player and do the same, with dust enough to buy a
listing from phone A. Keep a third browser with site data cleared for the guest
items (`DST-02`, `DST-21`, `DST-58`). Balances are banked against the person and
survive everything, so they do not need resetting between items — but note that
milling and selling are irreversible, so work down a list of spares you are
willing to lose. Device values are as defined in
[the protocol](README.md#devices-and-conditions); `second person` in this file
always means phone B driven by somebody else at the same time, and rows carrying
it cannot be run alone.

Not checkable by hand:

- That the balance is a sum over signed movements computed on every ask rather
  than a stored total, and that it is answered with a no-store header. Both are
  server properties; the phone sees the same number either way
  ([Arrive](../dust/dust.md#arrive)).
- That a balance cannot go negative because every spending call locks the
  player's row before reading the balance. The lock is not observable; only its
  outcome is, and that is covered by the race items in the shop and marketplace
  tables ([Edge cases](../dust/dust.md#edge-cases)).
- That deleting a player takes their dust with them and leaves no orphaned
  balance. Destructive, and there is no surface left to look at afterwards
  ([Edge cases](../dust/dust.md#edge-cases)).
- Whether the confirm text on the commissioner's switch naming the secret
  ladder's two ends is the most useful thing to say at that moment. The document
  says outright this is a product question rather than a defect
  ([Open questions](../dust/dust.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That the pill appears nowhere but the vault header. An absence across the whole
  source ([Open questions](../dust/dust.md#open-questions-and-verification)).

# Verification: cards

How to run this file: bring the surface up the way [the protocol](README.md)
says — `bun run dev` on a phone on the same network, or the deployed app, which
is the better choice for anything involving a second device — and confirm
`git rev-parse --short HEAD` reads `b46f330` first. A fresh state for this
cluster means clearing site data for the origin, which removes the `wwbh:` keys
these documents depend on (`wwbh:vault-favourites` and its shelf order twin
`wwbh:vault-layout`, `wwbh:trophy-seen`, `wwbh:sfx-muted`, `wwbh:guest-token`,
`wwbh:member-token`, `wwbh:member-name`, `wwbh:was-member`, `wwbh:guest-name`,
`wwbh:device-id`, `wwbh:trade-seen` and the `wwbh:v…` art-URL snapshots) and the
IndexedDB database `wwbh-cards`, which holds the collection and the day's pack
under the `pack-state` store at key `today`; `wwbh-combine` belongs to the timing
console and is irrelevant here. **Clear deliberately, not between items**: a new
guest gets a fresh daily secret and loses everything the old one held, and a
cleared `wwbh:trophy-seen` makes the next launch swallow every trophy ceremony
silently. Most items here need a collection, so budget league days: one pack a
day, one secret a day, and a streak that only builds on consecutive days. Start
each pass with reduced motion off, card sound unmuted, Tilt off, the browser's
motion permission not yet granted, the device clock automatic, the vault in its
default layout with every shelf open and the Name sort selected, and dust off
unless the row says otherwise. Device values in this file: **phone** is the real
surface and the default; **desktop** is a wide browser, used where a row needs
devtools, a keyboard, or the wide layout that puts six chips on one line;
**guest** is a browser with site data cleared and no player claimed; **member**
holds a member token claimed from a paper code; **account** is signed in with
email and password, which is what a milestone claim requires; **commissioner**
is an admin session, needed to grant, retire, rename or hide a card or a set;
**second device** is genuinely a second handset — a second tab shares storage
and IndexedDB, and several items turn on that difference, so where a row says
second tab it means a tab and where it says second device it means a phone;
**second person** is somebody else's member session, for trades; **offline** is
airplane mode on the handset rather than a devtools toggle; **reduced motion**
is the OS-level setting, not a control in the app; **gyroscope** is a real phone
with motion permission, and the permission prompt is itself an item; **race
day** marks items that can only honestly be checked while a combine is running.

Not checkable by hand:

- Which position the Favourites shelf takes when a previously emptied shelf comes back, on a device that has reordered its vault ([Open questions and verification](../cards/favourites.md#open-questions-and-verification)). It is a layout-merge question, and the answer depends on stored order the tester cannot see the shape of.
- Whether a card pinned against a previous combine reappears if that combine becomes active again ([Open questions and verification](../cards/favourites.md#open-questions-and-verification)) — it needs two combines and a device that pinned against the older one.
- That no other feature reads or writes the favourites key ([Open questions and verification](../cards/favourites.md#open-questions-and-verification)). A stray write would present as junk, which VLT-08 already covers; the absence of other writers is a source claim.
- Whether the star being dimmed rather than hidden-until-hover is the right call ([Modifiers](../cards/favourites.md#modifiers)) — a design decision, not a behaviour.

Not checkable by hand:

- Whether a stale "already revealed" flag is correctly ignored for a card minted just now ([Cancel and interrupt](../cards/the-daily-secret.md#cancel-and-interrupt)). It needs a resumed pack straddling a real league-midnight boundary, which is hours of waiting rather than a step.
- That the pull takes no input at all and the identity comes from a verified token ([What decides it](../cards/the-daily-secret.md#what-decides-it)) — a request-shape claim, checked by the server-function tests rather than by a screen.
- That two tabs tearing at once both get the same card, distinct from VLT-143's sequential case ([Edge cases](../cards/the-daily-secret.md#edge-cases)); genuinely simultaneous tears cannot be produced by hand.
- Whether the commissioner retiring a card mid-pull affects a pull already in flight ([Edge cases](../cards/the-daily-secret.md#edge-cases)) — a race, not a step.

- Whether a ceremony that opens over a pack still in its reveal composes cleanly ([Open questions and verification](../cards/collection-trophies.md#open-questions-and-verification)) — it needs a grant timed into somebody else's reveal.
- The timing of the beat between a secret's burst and the gold on a slow phone ([Open questions and verification](../cards/collection-trophies.md#open-questions-and-verification)); VLT-235 records the order, and the length of the gap is a design call.
- That the trophy is minted by the same database call that hands over the card, inside one lock ([The tap that starts something](../cards/collection-trophies.md#the-tap-that-starts-something)) — the observable half is that there is no state where you hold the last card and have no trophy, which only a forced failure could probe.
- A queue cut short by closing the app mid-queue ([Edge cases](../cards/collection-trophies.md#edge-cases)): the ceremonies are marked shown as they are queued, so the loss is by design and leaves nothing to see.

Not checkable by hand:

- Every rung above day 3 in a single pass. The ladder is 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 consecutive league days, and there is no way to build a run other than to live it; VLT-269 checks whichever floor the tester can actually reach.
- A rebuilt streak re-earning the whole ladder, and claims being keyed to the run's first day ([Edge cases](../cards/pack-streaks.md#edge-cases)) — it needs a run, a break, and a second run.
- A guest history merged in behind an existing run, pushing a run's first day backwards ([Edge cases](../cards/pack-streaks.md#edge-cases)); it needs two overlapping histories built over days.
- "Nothing in the catalogue to give" ([Edge cases](../cards/pack-streaks.md#edge-cases)) — it needs every secret card retired at the moment a rung is cashed, and even then it must not file a claim.
- A run past a hundred days printing no promise ([Edge cases](../cards/pack-streaks.md#edge-cases)).
- Whether a phone whose local day turns before the league's advances the streak twice ([Open questions and verification](../cards/pack-streaks.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
